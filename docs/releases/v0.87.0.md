# denoize v0.87.0

v0.87.0 implements Stage 34: bounded continuous speech separation and
anonymous diarization into lossless meeting speaker tracks.

## Added

- Added the dedicated finite package-v2 meeting graph contract: fixed mono or
  authenticated fixed-array input, at most eight separated tracks,
  `track_activity` probabilities, and explicit global unknown probabilities.
- Added exact bounded window-permutation assignment with correlation and
  runner-up margins. Every ambiguous stitch is exposed as unknown rather than
  hidden behind aggregate separation scores.
- Added presentation-sample active, uncertain, overlap, and unknown regions,
  consecutive-active publication holds, duration/finite/peak gates, and a
  required `unassigned` residual that reconstructs the mono reference within
  `1e-12`.
- Added `denoize meeting-speakers`. Its lossless WAV channels are the published
  anonymous tracks followed by the unassigned residual; its report is closed
  and path-free.
- Added optional Stage 29 consent-bound labels. Raw enrollment and speaker
  embeddings are rejected and never retained.
- Added signed 12-stratum promotion evidence covering real meetings,
  single-/multi-channel use, overlap, track swaps, speaker count, unknown
  assignment, tcpWER, DER/JER, listening, licenses, and privacy.
- Added three JSON Schemas, semantic contract tests, generated CLI reference,
  crate packaging checks, release-asset verification, and documentation.

## Safety boundary

No separator, diarizer, ASR graph, meeting corpus, or checkpoint is bundled.
The exact graph, numerical vectors, source/checkpoint identity, licenses,
training datasets, resource limits, evaluation corpus/license manifests, and
promotion results must be authenticated before input audio is opened.
Transcription and automatic identity inference are not product outputs.

## Research basis

The architecture follows NOTSOFAR-1's separation of CSS, diarization, and ASR
and the modular speaker-count/EEND-VC/TS-VAD/beamforming analysis of Kamo et al.
(CHiME-8, 2025). Paper results select interfaces and evaluation strata; they do
not authorize redistribution of code, data, or weights.
